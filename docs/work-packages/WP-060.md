- Spec version: 12.4.0
- Stage: Planning and dry run (M2)
- Objective: Deliver the pure planning library the Section 14 charter
  names: a deterministic, side-effect-free computation (PLAN-001) from a
  captured snapshot, capability answers, and a typed request to an

## Boundary — surfaces this package must not build

Each prohibition names its governing authority.

- **No step outside the sole constructor.** Every mutating step is
  `PlanStep::mutating` over the capture's authenticated facts — the same
  closure, the same acknowledgment law (ADR-0012, ADR-0018). The planner
  sequences and solves; it never re-implements, caches, or bypasses the
  refusal machinery, and a capability answer cannot admit a step the
  closure refuses (CAP-007 both ways).
- **No verdicts, no facts, no snapshots of its own.** Protection is the
  domain's; capability answers are WP-050's; captured snapshots arrive
  decoded from the typed boundary. The simulated topology is built
  through `TopologySnapshot::assemble` with `SnapshotKind::Simulated` —
  the schema string that can never be a planning base or satisfy a
  PLAN-006 comparison, structurally (3c's rule).
- **No register answer by construction.** Every gate this assignment
  named is now cleared: SI-19 (ADR-0022, spec 12.0.0), SI-15
  (ADR-0023, 12.1.0), SI-16 (ADR-0024, 12.2.0), SI-17 (ADR-0025,
  12.3.0), and SI-24 (ADR-0026, 12.4.0). Their increments open under
  recorded decisions rather than under guesses — implementing a
  decided answer is not answering. SI-15's delivered refusal was
  replaced by ADR-0023's decided behavior in increment 5, SI-16's
  backup family landed as increment 7, and SI-17's withheld
  combination was replaced by ADR-0025's derived criterion in
  increment 8 — each unlock implemented its recorded decision rather
  than an open question, and none was silently answered.
  The principle outlives the empty list: any newly filed gate is
  conservative-refused, never silently answered.
- **No hashed-body change.** The plan body schema is WP-010's. A
  vocabulary this package delivers that must enter the body (PLAN-005's
  cancellation class, eventually PLAN-008's reversal linkage) lands as a
  jointly-sequenced WP-010 schema change under WP-010's grant, with the
  MODEL-003 version discipline that file owns.
- **No platform reach, no apply, no elevation.** Pure computation over
  supplied values, exactly like the engine it consumes.

## PLAN-005 cancellation-class decision — recorded 2026-08-12

The beyond-list above holds PLAN-005's full vocabulary to "the shape
WP-035's route-decision precedent set": a recorded choice before the
increment opens. This section is that record.

**The three routes, and what each costs.** (1) **Derive the class from
`InterruptionProfile`** — rejected outright: spec 12.3.0 records
cannot-stop (PLAN-005's `non-cancellable`) and cannot-unwind
(`irreversible-after-start`) as independent facts in both directions,
and a derivation re-couples them. The counterexamples are concrete:
`Wipe` and `Shrink` share `UnrestorableIntermediate` yet differ in
stoppability, and the journaled chunk copy has interruption windows yet
stops safely at its declared checkpoints. (2) **The
all-non-cancellable floor with no family table** — rejected as the
vacuous shape: it discharges PLAN-005's letter while deleting the
vocabulary's point, leaves ACC-012's family claim unstated, and records
no place where a family earns its class. (3) **A per-family stated
declaration over a conservative floor** — 12.3.0's closing prose
already says flag assignment to concrete step families "is each
building package's testable declaration"; the class follows the same
discipline.

**The choice: route (3).** A family claims `cancellable` or
`checkpoint-cancellable` only on decided text or a measured mechanism;
the floor is `non-cancellable`. The asymmetry is the safety argument:
claiming less cancellation than reality costs only convenience — the
UI offers nothing — while claiming more makes the UI offer a stop the
executor cannot honor, exactly the violation PLAN-005's second
sentence forbids.

**The table this decision states today.**

| Family | Class | Backing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Move`, `Copy` | `checkpoint-cancellable` | PART-005's normative journaled chunk copy with a durable progress map; ACC-012's "stops at the declared checkpoint". Stated for the family before the planner emits it — the increment-8 `InterruptionProfile` precedent. |
| Every family the planner emits today (the entry writes, the sized create, grow, shrink, wipe, the table repair) | `non-cancellable` | The floor. No measured safe-stop story exists for any of them, and no decided text grants one; the executor does not exist yet. |

**What this makes true, exactly.** Every step in a version-4 body
carries one of PLAN-005's three words; the UI's must-not-offer rule
becomes derivable from the carried class rather than guessed; the
executor's cancel-honor points key off it (Section 8's safe point,
EXE-004's acknowledgment — both that side's obligations, not this
table's). `CancelClaim` and ADR-0025's coupling rule are untouched,
and the recorded independence becomes visible in the vocabulary: the
atomic entry write is `non-cancellable` yet unflagged, and a flagged
family may later earn `checkpoint-cancellable` without touching its
flag.

**Revisit conditions.** A measured safe-stop story for the overwrite
wipe — the natural first candidate, long-running and structurally
consistent at every prefix, but its mechanism could be a hardware
sanitize command that cannot stop, so the claim waits for the executor
era's measurement; a checkpointed story for the shrink's in-place
transform, if one appears; any new operation family, which arrives
with its own stated class. Each earning updates this decision's table
in a reviewed change — never a silent constant edit.
