# WP-035: Read-only CLI chassis and evidence instrument

- Spec version: 4.2.0
- Stage: Evidence (M0.5)
- Objective: Build the unprivileged, read-only, offline `partman inspect`
  chassis and fixture-replay evidence instrument, plus dependency
  availability/version facts, and take the named register measurements:
  SI-33's media-change-counter liveness experiment and SI-35's loop-device and
  Windows partition-list measurements. Production native adapters and product
  answers about what a host exposes belong to WP-W100/WP-L100/WP-M100; this
  package records only bounded measurement evidence. Dependency-usability and
  per-target capability verdicts belong to WP-050. This chassis requires no
  decision from any register item gating WP-010 increment 3, is forbidden the
  domain surfaces those items gate, records per-field provenance, and never
  synthesizes a verdict from what it observed. The package also preregisters,
  attempts, and records the specifically named non-elevated macOS and real-
  partitioned-Linux
  observability rows required before the next WP-010 blocker-resolution rounds
  freeze identity, topology, or verdict bytes.
- Requirement IDs: SAFE-004, SAFE-005 (fail-closed refusal posture only; no
  write operation exists here to disable), SAFE-006, SEC-007, MODEL-003
  (schema versions on this package's own JSON surfaces; the domain-payload
  regime remains WP-010's), MODEL-004 (precursor observation records only:
  adapter, version, method, and ADR-C4's outcome vocabulary as the ADR wrote
  it — observed, with absence as a value; unavailable; failed — the hashed
  envelope these eventually live in is WP-010's to deliver), CLI-002
  (export-diagnostics only), CLI-005 (documented; provisional within major
  version 0), CLI-008, INV-006, INV-007, CAP-004, FS-007 (inputs only:
  static technology-limit facts; the blocked-reason capability surface is
  WP-050's)
- Increment 6 measurement-only requirement IDs: SAFE-001, SAFE-002, SAFE-003,
  HLP-002, INV-002, INV-003, MODEL-005, PLAN-006. These identify the disposable-
  target, privilege, identity, native-designator, partition-observation, hashed-
  placement, and freshness facts the protocol measures. They do not claim
  delivery of a platform adapter, domain type, helper, topology hash, plan
  representation, or capability verdict.
- Prerequisites: WP-000, WP-020, repository issue #94 for anything loop-backed
- Test tier: T1, unprivileged. No test opens a block device, and no test
  launches a roster tool: the doctor's I/O goes through an injected launcher
  seam, so WP-035's direct test subprocess set stays `git` and the
  compile-time-selected `cargo`. The real-launch tests probe Git at one
  reviewed absolute path for both a successful `--version` and an intentional
  nonzero exit; Cargo remains the structural-dependency oracle.
  The SI-33/SI-35 measurements and increment 6 are operator-run, read-only
  experiments recorded in `docs/quality/observability.md` — the SI-28
  hardware-confirmation precedent — not tests and not repository commands;
  the loop-backed portion is additionally gated on repository issue #94
  (nothing binds `/dev/loopN` to a verified handle until that closes).
- Acceptance scenarios: none directly assigned. ACC-009 (missing tool) depends
  on CAP-004 work delivered here but is not claimed by it.

## Boundary — surfaces this package must not build

Each prohibition names its governing open issue, accepted decision,
requirement, or future owner. Represented inspect fields and the reserved
`inventory`, `topology`, and `capabilities` commands refuse with typed
references — `{"strength": {"state": "not-established", "gate": "SI-28"}}` —
never merely an exit code or a stderr string. Future mutation and planning
command words are absent from the parser and remain usage refusals; hash and
plan types are absent from the crate rather than represented as refusal values.

- **No `IdentityStrength`** — SI-28. (Formerly "SI-28, SI-35"; SI-35 resolved
  in spec 8.0.0 by ADR-0014, and the strength gate is SI-28's alone now.
  SI-28 is Mitigated-open, reclassified off the WP-010 increment-3 gate with
  its floor in force — which moves nothing here.) Recording what a device
  reported is not asserting what the identifier identifies, and that
  attribution question is what SI-28 leaves open. The current replay adapter
  emits no identifiers; any future adapter observation admitted through this
  chassis may expose only raw identifier strings labelled with the reporting
  interface, never a strength.
- **No ADR-C3 partition-table state, no table checksum** — a standing
  decision now, not an open question: SI-35 resolved in spec 8.0.0 by
  ADR-0014, which makes the privileged helper the sole author of table state
  from its own raw-sector parser; the unprivileged client emits none on any
  platform. The prohibition is unchanged and no longer interim; its authority
  moved.
- **No typed Section 5 node, no `TopologySnapshot`, no artifact hash, no
  plan** — formerly SI-27, SI-34, SI-35; all three are Resolved (SI-35 in
  spec 8.0.0 by ADR-0014, SI-34 in 9.0.0 by ADR-0016, SI-27 in 11.1.0 by
  ADR-0019), and the typed surfaces those decisions authorize now exist in
  `crates/domain` under WP-010's increment 3. The prohibition is unchanged:
  those types stay out of this package until an increment is granted to
  consume them. Two mechanical guards enforce this, and their
  exact reach is stated so the sentence cannot rot into an overclaim: the
  shipped dependency closure is exactly `partman-launcher` — the SAFE-004
  mechanism crate, itself dependency-free, both facts asserted through
  `cargo metadata`, so no hash or plan implementation can arrive from
  outside the crate, transitively — WP-010's included (the closure was
  empty until the 2026-08-20 launcher move; the widening was the
  launcher-home round's found cost, priced and accepted), and the
  output type carries a Tier-1 compile-time ambiguity proof that it does not
  implement `Hash`.
  What neither guard can see — `std`'s own hashers used deliberately
  in-crate — is a named review obligation. Increment 1 is not complete until
  both guards exist and defeating either fails the suite.
- **No stable device handle** — ADR-0019 now, plus the unconsumed types it
  authorized. (Formerly "via SI-12's multipath question", then SI-27's alone
  once SI-12 resolved in spec 4.3.0; SI-27 resolved in spec 11.1.0 by
  ADR-0019, whose derived positional addresses landed as WP-010's
  `model::naming`. The prohibition is unchanged: nothing wires those types
  into this chassis, which still ships no stable handle.) Selectors are
  session-local indexes or the platform's own path, documented as unstable
  across runs. This is a real product cost and it is the cost of not
  guessing.
- **No claim that two paths are or are not the same device** — a standing
  decision now, not an open question: ADR-0011 (spec 4.3.0) made
  no-cross-path-sameness-inference the product rule. The prohibition is
  unchanged; its authority moved.
- **No protection verdict** for ZFS, Storage Spaces, LDM, Fusion, or Apple
  sealed system — ADR-0018 now, not an open register item. (Formerly "SI-11
  (inputs SI-29, SI-30, SI-37)"; SI-11 resolved in spec 11.0.0 by ADR-0018,
  SI-29 and SI-30 resolved within it, and SI-37 reclassified open at Later,
  off the gate.) The Section 2.1 verdicts themselves and the
  unrepresentable-by-construction enforcement axis were already ADR-0012's;
  the protection closure and fail-closed residual are decided by ADR-0018 and
  implemented as WP-010's protection layer, and this package builds neither —
  the verdict surface stays absent from this chassis either way.
- **No per-target capability verdict, and no CAP-003 status values.** The
  status vocabulary and its reason enum belong to WP-050's capability engine;
  the register's round-three record fixes the closed reason enum as the home
  for gated-state reporting. Technology-limit facts (FS-007 inputs) are in
  scope; a verdict about a target is not.
- **No `plan`, `validate`, `dry-run`, `apply-plan`, `status`, `resume`,
  `cancel`** — there is no honest partial `plan`.
- **No unversioned JSON, and no domain payloads.** Every JSON surface this
  package emits — refusal values, progress events, doctor and diagnostics
  envelopes — carries a schema version this package owns, documented as
  provisional within major version 0 (MODEL-003). Inventory, topology, and
  capability payloads are absent from output entirely; a request for them
  refuses with a typed value naming the gate. CLI-001's stable-JSON obligation
  stays with the packages that own those surfaces.

A read-only inspector satisfies SI-28's interim conservative floor vacuously.
The inspector's vacuous compliance may not be cited toward narrowing that
floor; the SI-33 measurements increment 5 takes are register evidence and are
cited there, not here.

### Increment 6 — round-four platform evidence

Increment 6 may preregister and execute read-only, primarily non-elevated
measurements on:

- macOS, using structured IOKit/IOMedia observations and structured `diskutil`
  or APFS output; and
- a disposable, non-WSL distro Linux VM, including explicitly disposable USB
  passthrough where a real device tree or partitioned physical medium is
  required.

The preregistered matrix is fixed to the evidence the register names:

- SI-34's raw client-versus-direct-probe freshness projection for the stale-
  signature case. The record may identify a fact as protection-relevant only
  where the existing register already does so; it may not compute a final
  protection verdict or exercise reject, proceed, or journal behavior;
- Part 6 precondition 1's separate unprivileged-client and privileged-helper
  projections for platform-applicable mdraid, LUKS2, ZFS, LVM2, and APFS
  signatures or membership facts;
- Part 6 precondition 2's platform-applicable native designators and their raw
  byte forms, sources, privilege, stability, and collision behavior;
- real-partitioned-Linux hardware rows taken through explicitly disposable
  physical-media passthrough rather than represented by a virtual disk; and
- the macOS Fusion membership and shape claim only when representative hardware
  exists; otherwise that cell remains `not established`.

Every matrix cell must preregister its command or API, privilege class,
distinguishing condition, invalidation conditions, and permitted result
vocabulary before execution. Every Linux row additionally records effective
UID, effective capabilities, relevant group memberships, and the tested device
node's ownership, mode, and ACLs. The ordinary-client baseline must lack raw-
block access; a `disk`-group, capability-bearing, or ACL-granted run is a
separately labelled projection and may not substitute for that baseline.
Recording `not established` is an honest result, not completion: the next
affected WP-010 blocker-resolution round remains blocked until every row its
register gate requires is established.

Privileged comparison legs must be separately labelled, narrowly justified,
and must not be used to claim that an unprivileged product path can observe the
same fact. Elevated legs may run only inside a disposable VM or hosted test
environment satisfying SAFE-001 and SAFE-002; no privileged comparison may run
on the user's ordinary macOS host. A Proxmox VM may satisfy the non-WSL kernel
requirement, but a virtual disk alone may not be represented as real-hardware
evidence. Physical USB media must be explicitly provisioned and labelled as
fixture media; automount and repair are disabled, and a no-write/write-intent
assertion is recorded before passthrough.

Each protocol must be preregistered before execution, assert and record its
privilege state, preserve a complete retrievable transcript outside the
repository, record a content hash for that transcript, perform an independent
read-back where applicable, and state explicit validity and falsification
conditions. The committed record identifies the archive locator and custodian,
hash algorithm and digest, byte length, capture-script digest, OS, kernel and
tool versions, exit statuses, fixture digest or anonymized device-role mapping,
and a successful fetch-and-rehash result. Raw diagnostic output, machine-
specific identifiers, and secrets must not be committed. Only normalized
observations and limitations enter the repository.

Unavailable hardware or topology remains `not established`; it must not be
inferred from a nearby platform or synthetic substitute. In particular,
degraded Fusion behavior remains unestablished unless suitable hardware
exists. These measurements are design evidence only and do not satisfy any
platform-adapter acceptance criterion.

Issue #94 remains the prerequisite for WP-035's loop-backed SI-35 leg. It is not
a prerequisite for unrelated read-only macOS or real-hardware Linux

## Increment 10 route decision — deferral, recorded 2026-08-08

The grant above opens increment 10 "only after a recorded choice among its
three named routes." This section is that record, and the choice is the
route that builds nothing.

**The three routes, and what each costs.** No Windows enumeration route is
simultaneously dependency-free, `unsafe`-free, and clean against the
tool-invocation rules. (1) **WMI/CIM** needs FFI, which `apps/cli` cannot
host under the workspace `unsafe` denial; hosting it in a separate crate
breaks the empty-dependency-closure guard and adds SAFE-009 reviewed-unsafe
surface. (2) **PowerShell** adds a shell to the SAFE-004 roster — the
requirement whose first sentence is "no shell strings" — and its output
still needs a JSON reader, so it pays a parser cost on top of the roster
widening. (3) **Deferral to WP-W100**, the shape spec 6.1.0 built the M0.5
gate to accept: the platform ships its reach declaration and a typed
`not-implemented` answer naming the recorded decision that defers it.

**The choice: route (3), deferral.** Recommended by the 2026-08-05
handoff's prior analysis and the 2026-08-08 decision briefs; implemented on
Nate McBride's direction to proceed with the briefs' recommendations
(2026-08-08). Both live routes would spend a Section 3 constraint or a
bought structural guard on an interim surface WP-W100 replaces anyway;
deferral spends nothing that cannot be recovered.

**What this makes true, exactly.** Increment 10 is closed as deferred and
never opens as implementation. The Windows `inspect` answer stays the typed
`not-implemented` value naming WP-W100 and now also names this decision
in-band; the Windows reach reference names this decision instead of a
pending increment. WP-W100's Section 14 row is untouched — deferral removes
no scope from it and adds none. M0.5's exit is unaffected: the gate was
extended in 6.1.0 deliberately so that a platform in exactly this position
does not hold the milestone. The record sweep that closes the package
(increment 11) carries this decision into the status surfaces.

**Revisit conditions.** WP-W100 opening; or a spec change that gives
`apps/cli` a Windows route without the constraint triangle — either a safe,
vetted dependency policy decision for a CIM client, or a structured
non-shell Windows interface this package could read under its existing
guards. Until one of those exists, the honest Windows answer is a decision,
not a promise.

## The launcher move — 2026-08-20, on the launcher-home round's option A

**Delivered 2026-08-20.** The SAFE-004 launch mechanism — `ToolLauncher`,
`SystemLauncher`, the bounded spawn/drain/kill core and `ProbeOutcome` —
moved verbatim from `apps/cli/src/doctor.rs` to the new workspace crate
`crates/launcher` (`partman-launcher`, this package's, reserved by the
2026-08-20 Governance act), with one contract change the round required in
the same move: **`launch` takes a caller-stated deadline beside the
caller-stated output bound**, and the crate holds no policy value at all —
no roster, no bound, no time limit. The CLI keeps every policy: its
`ROSTER`, its output bounds, and `DOCTOR_TIME_LIMIT` (the same five
seconds this binary has always applied, now stated at each of its three
launch sites — the doctor's fixed `--version` probe and the macOS
adapter's two diskutil invocations). `probe_version` left the trait: it
was `launch` with fixed policy, and policy lives with callers now.

The guards moved with their subjects rather than rotting: the CLI's
dependency-closure guard became
`the_shipped_dependency_closure_is_exactly_the_launcher` — the found cost
the launcher-home round had not priced, put to the decision owner
2026-08-20 and accepted — asserting through `cargo metadata` that the
shipped closure is `partman-launcher` alone **and** that the launcher
crate itself depends on nothing, which closes the closure at depth two;
the env-clear and env-read pins now scan the mechanism's source in its
new home; the process-constructor pin inverted — every CLI source is now
free of direct process construction, the one constructor pinned inside
`partman-launcher` by its own test; and the fixed-probe pin asserts the
doctor's single `.launch(` call spells literal `--version` with
`DOCTOR_TIME_LIMIT`. The crate carries seven tests of its own, including
the two the parameters made provable for the first time: a one-byte
output bound refuses `git --version` as over-output-limit, and an
already-expired deadline is killed and reported timed-out — the caller's
bounds, enforced, not constants trusted. Rust: owes its WP-020 sitting,
named in the delivering pull request.
