## The question, and what has changed since it was filed

SI-34 asks whether the helper's derived protection verdict is frozen
into the hashed body. Round two said yes on a universal premise
(every client/helper asymmetry is roster identity) that the
stale-signature fixture refuted; the filing's three options — clamp the
projection so both observers agree (a), drop the verdict from the body
(b), or freeze a cross-privilege freshness projection plus a monotone
floor the helper may only tighten (c) — all answer one underlying
problem: **two observers authoring one body**. A client-derived bound
snapshot and a helper's recomputation could disagree on unchanged
hardware, making PLAN-006 unsatisfiable; (a) blinds the helper to fix
it, (b) un-authenticates the verdict, (c) builds comparator-and-floor
machinery to bridge it.

**ADR-0014 removed the two-observer world.** Since spec 8.0.0, the
snapshot whose hash an authorized plan binds is helper-produced at
validation; PLAN-006's comparison is helper-authored against
helper-recomputed, one observer class; and MODEL-005 carries a named
authoring-at-validation verb — deliberately closed to partition-table
state "so 'the helper writes some body fields' cannot creep by
analogy." The closure invites exactly one kind of extension: a decided
one, through a register resolution with its own round. This is that
shape.

**And the evidence ground completed.** The entry's currency note still
says M10 is `not yet taken` and the both-views stale-signature
comparison "cannot be measured there until M10 exists" — stale twice
over, load-bearingly: M10 was taken 2026-08-05, its second-reader
readback discharged 2026-08-08, and it measured the macOS half of
exactly the comparison the first evidence element names (the client's
projection byte-identical to blank; the helper's digests distinct, the
stale-pair fixture differing at head and tail). The Linux increment 6
matrix measured the rest on a real device tree: L10's both-directions
asymmetry (single-answer interfaces report exactly the stale signature,
the enumerating probe reports both) and the double-capture byte
stability of the helper's own reads. The currency fix travels with
whatever this round produces.

## Recommendation: the verdict is body content, helper-authored at validation

Option (c)'s successor rather than any filed option as written — (a)'s
placement with ADR-0014's mechanism instead of (a)'s clamp:

1. **Placement: hashed body.** The verdict a user authorizes is in the
   bytes they authorize — ADR-C2's charter ("bound device identities
   and strength are body; the authorization names these targets")
   extends to the safety decision about those targets.
2. **Authorship: the helper, at validation** — MODEL-005's authoring
   set reopens by decision and closes again at exactly two entries:
   partition-table state and the derived protection verdict, both
   fields only the helper can derive, both stamped during validate-plan
   before HLP-003 binds authorization, both recomputed at revalidation
   and before the first write. A client-authored value for either never
   validates. Extending the closed set is a semantic change to 8.0.0's
   sentence: **major when it lands.**
3. **Divergence rejects — strictly, because the stricter rule is
   already normative.** At apply, HLP-002 re-discovers and the helper
   recomputes; any verdict change within the bound target topology is a
   body mismatch, and SAFE-003/PLAN-006 already reject the plan on
   changed identity or topology. No floor, no monotone-tightening
   protocol, no journaled-continue arm: those were (c)'s machinery for
   safely *relaxing* strictness across two observers, and with one
   author there is nothing to relax. Evidence outside the bound targets
   never entered the bound snapshot and cannot mismatch it — PLAN-006's
   own scoping, "target topology," does the work (c) built a projection
   artifact for.
4. **What dissolves, named.** The monotonicity proof obligation (no
   client verdict exists to tighten — the same dissolution SI-35's
   helper-only move produced); the freshness-projection artifact (its
   job was cross-observer comparability; freshness itself stays owned
   by PLAN-006, PLAN-007's windows, revalidation, and SI-33's open
   route); and the client-claims-`permitted` evidence element (no
   client claim is representable, so the helper's `refused` cannot lose
   to one — by construction rather than by contest).
5. **What this deliberately does not decide.** The verdict's internal
   shape — total fail-closed semantics, PART-014 classification, the
   reason vocabulary, the Storage Spaces and sealed-volume cases — is
   SI-11's round, untouched. Node naming and the edges verdicts attach
   to are SI-27's, and the entry's own correction stands: moving or
   keeping the verdict changes SI-27's protection-specific burden only.
   SI-28/SI-33 are untouched.

**Evidence clause, discharged at honest scope, SI-35's precedent:**

- *Measured now:* the stale-signature both-views facts on real Linux
  (L10) and macOS (M7 + M10), with helper read-stability
  double-captured — these establish why no client-comparable projection
  exists and why single authorship is the design rather than a
  preference.
- *Write-path elements* — helper-only fact changing protection rejects
  pre-write with a structured divergence; helper-only fact changing
  nothing proceeds journaled (which under this design narrows to:
  out-of-target evidence never blocks) — cannot exist before increment
  3 and land as named obligations on the first write-capable increment,
  in the resolution banner, beside SI-35's end-to-end refusal
  obligation.

## The adversarial round

**Attack 1 — "the refutation was intra-helper too: `wipefs` sees both
signatures while root `blkid -p` sees one, so 'one author' still has
two eyes."** The round's best attack, and it produced obligation rather
than retreat: single authorship makes the PLAN-006 problem disappear
only if the helper's *derivation contract* is named and deterministic —
which evidence set, which probes, in what precedence — exactly as
ADR-0014 named the raw-sector parser rather than "run a privileged
tool." The resolution must bind the verdict to a named helper evidence
contract whose re-probe stability is measured (the double-capture rows
exist; more may be owed), and SI-11's shape round inherits that
contract requirement. An unnamed evidence set would be round two's
universal premise wearing a beard.

**Attack 2 — "8.0.0 closed the authoring set one change ago; reopening
it now proves the closure was decoration."** Refuted by reading the
closure's purpose: it forbids *creep by analogy*, and its sentence
exists precisely so that an extension is a register decision with a
round — this one. The alternative placement (out-of-body, option (b))
is also priced and also available: the fork's four conditions govern
any draft that prefers it. The round weighed it: (b) pays
name-what-authorization-stops-committing-to for no gain single
authorship doesn't already deliver, and un-authenticates the one value
the user most needs bound.

**Attack 3 — "always-reject is harsher than (c); a device arriving
mid-window would kill plans (c) would have journaled past."** Refuted
by scope: PLAN-006 compares the bound *target* topology; an arrival
outside the bound set never entered the snapshot. Within the bound set,
always-reject is SAFE-003's existing text, not new strictness — (c)'s
journaled-continue was a *relaxation* proposal needing floor machinery
to stay safe, and nothing today demands it. If write-path experience
ever does, that is a future decision with its own evidence, recorded
here as foreseen rather than foreclosed.

**Attack 4 — "increment 3 needs the verdict type; this resolves
placement and leaves the type to SI-11, so what did increment 3 gain?"**
What SI-34 was filed to give it: the hash-visible fact. Where the
verdict lives, who writes it, and when it is recomputed are what the
Section 5 types must encode; the variant set inside it is SI-11's, and
ADR-0012/ADR-0014 set the precedent that placement decisions land
separately from shape rounds precisely so types stop being hostage to
both at once.

**Attack 5 — "the display cost compounds: clients now show neither
table state nor protection before validation."** Accepted knowingly,
third time as policy: ADR-0013 priced it for detection, ADR-0014 for
table state, and the honest client surface remains observations, reach,
and pending-validation — the moment a user most wants a per-device
safety answer is the moment no unprivileged observer can honestly give
one, and the measured macOS rows (M7: the client cannot see the stale
signature, the live filesystem, or the difference between them) are the
proof by demonstration.

## Rejected, and why — for the record the resolution will carry

- **(a) as filed** — clamp both observers to an agreed projection:
  blinds the helper to evidence it holds; obsolete now that no client
  observer authors anything.
- **(b) drop from body** — un-authenticates the safety decision and
  pays the fork's condition 3 to lose the property single authorship
  keeps for free.
- **(c) as filed** — projection-plus-floor machinery whose two open
  dependencies (projection membership, monotonicity proof) were real
  costs paid to bridge two authors; with one author both dissolve, and
  keeping the machinery would be complexity commemorating a dead
  constraint.
