# SI-33 round — 2026-08-09

**Status: a recommendation for Nate's decision, adversarially reviewed.
It decides nothing.** SI-33's liveness precondition was discharged
2026-08-05 with three declared limits; this round designs inside those
limits rather than around them. Untracked session artifact
(`docs/reviews/**`, WP-000).

## The question, and the measured walls around it

SI-33 asks for a continuity witness for media that cannot be told
apart: bind the plan not to what the target *reports* but to evidence
the medium was never exchanged between plan creation and apply. The S4
sittings made the need concrete beyond argument: a same-model reader
pair presents **one identical placeholder serial at every
serial-bearing layer**, a card exchange is invisible at every serial
surface, and unit continuity across the exchange is unverifiable
unprivileged. For that population, the plan/swap/apply vector —
seconds apart, one attach session — is undetectable by any identity
comparison, because every identity field lies identically.

The measured walls, each recorded before data existed:

1. **The ceiling.** Prompt counter movement cannot be attributed to
   exchange-synchronous detection; the strongest recordable positive is
   *"no staleness observed under these conditions."*
2. **The bound.** One reader, one bridge, one build, slot-exchange
   family. The liveness pass generalizes to nothing by itself.
3. **The non-monotonicity.** A reading decreased across a boundary
   containing a timestamped PnP arrival; re-arrival resets the counter
   to its epoch floor. An equality-only witness is unsafe; the epoch
   must be characterized — and it was: the storage-node PDO name
   qualified as an unprivileged epoch signal (ContainerId and the
   USB-node name refuted), cross-epoch readings incomparable by
   construction.

And the filing's own trap, which shapes everything: a witness that is
*evaluable but stale* fails open in exactly the vector it exists for,
"worse than no witness, because it converts an admitted gap into a
false assurance."

## Recommendation: a refusal input, never an assurance

Six clauses:

**1. The witness is a per-target field of SAFE-003's identity record**:
an epoch token plus a counter reading, taken and verified by the helper
at validation like every other record field. It is **not** a
MODEL-005 authoring-set entry — the counter was measured on zero-access
unprivileged handles, so this is a client-readable field the helper
re-derives and verifies, the same class as a serial, not a field only
the helper can produce. The authoring set stays closed at its two
entries; no third-entry-in-three-rounds erosion.

**2. Present only where it means something**: on exchange-capable
targets, on platforms whose signal-plus-epoch apparatus is qualified —
the ADR-0013 reach pattern applied to a witness. Windows'
`IOCTL_STORAGE_CHECK_VERIFY2` counter with the storage-node PDO epoch
token is the one qualified apparatus today; Linux and macOS analogues
are unmeasured and therefore absent, and absence of the field is the
status quo, not a regression — those targets keep the conservative
handling they have now.

**3. Comparison semantics, exactly as the measurements dictate.**
Readings are comparable **iff** the epoch token is unchanged **and**
the apply-time value is not below the stamp. Then:
- same epoch, value equal → **`no-exchange-observed`** — the ceiling's
  words, deliberately, and the strongest word the vocabulary contains;
- same epoch, value moved → exchange may have occurred: for covered
  targets this **is** SAFE-003's "identity has changed" and the plan
  rejects before the first write;
- epoch token changed, **or value decreased within a token** (a reset
  the token failed to witness — attack 3's finding) → incomparable:
  for covered targets, reject.

**4. `no-exchange-observed` never relaxes anything.** No plan proceeds
on the witness that would not have proceeded without it; the field only
adds refusals, so a stale counter on unmeasured hardware costs exactly
the assurance that was never claimed. This is the fail-closed inversion
of the filing's trap: negative-only use is safe on hardware nobody
measured, because false-stale ⇒ unchanged ⇒ nothing claimed, while
movement is always a genuine event stream fact.

**5. SI-28's floor is not relaxed, and the route stays honestly open.**
The upside SI-33 was filed hoping for — unchanged witness justifying
lighter confirmation — is unsound off the measured apparatus, by the
recorded bound. The relaxation is a named revisit condition requiring
its own evidence: an apparatus-qualification mechanism (per-family
qualification data, or an in-session user-mediated exchange ritual
whose counter movement demonstrates liveness on the very hardware in
question) and its own round. SI-33 resolves delivering the safety half
— detection where every identifier lies — and defers the convenience
half with a name on it.

**6. Placement falls out of clause 1**: the identity record is body
content by ADR-C2's standing rule, so the witness is hashed with the
record it belongs to. Body-stability holds within an epoch (no media
events, no movement — the double-read stability rows); an epoch
boundary during PLAN-007's validity window changes the body — and for
the covered population that *is* the correct outcome, continuity being
genuinely unknowable across the boundary. Strongly-identified
removables are untouched: SAFE-003's replug allowance governs them and
the witness field is scoped away from where identity can already
distinguish. The register note: SI-33 was classed non-hash-visible
while placement was open; this resolution makes it hash-visible through
placement, and the row is corrected rather than left contradicting the
decision.

## The adversarial round

**Attack 1 — "the field's mere presence invites consumers to read
`no-exchange-observed` as continuity; the false assurance arrives by
osmosis."** Sustained, and answered in the vocabulary itself: the
outcome set is closed
(`no-exchange-observed` / `exchange-observed` / `incomparable` /
`unavailable`), its strongest word is the ceiling's own phrase, no
`continuous` or `verified` exists to reach for, and the spec text
carries the ADR-0013-shaped consumer rule: a consumer MUST NOT treat
`no-exchange-observed` as evidence of continuity. The trap the filing
named is a naming problem before it is a mechanism problem.

**Attack 2 — "a reboot mid-window kills every pending removable plan."**
For the covered population, yes, and correctly: across an epoch
boundary, continuity for media indistinguishable by every identifier is
unknowable, and proceeding would be the exact gap the witness exists to
close. For everything else the answer is scoping, not mercy: strong
identity keeps its replug allowance, fixed disks never carry the field.

**Attack 3 — "S1's close/reopen survival was one apparatus; elsewhere a
reopen might reset the counter without a PnP re-arrival, and the epoch
token would vouch for a comparison it shouldn't."** The round's best
attack; it added the decrease-within-token arm to clause 3. A reset the
token fails to witness presents as a decrease, decreases are
incomparable, incomparable rejects for covered targets. The failure
mode of an unmeasured apparatus is a spurious refusal, never a false
pass.

**Attack 4 — "defer SI-33; the floor is unrelaxed anyway, so increment
3 could type the record without it."** Rejected on both counts: the
record type must decide the field's existence now or pay a hashed-schema
major later, and the safety delivery is real today — the S4 pair's
plan/swap/apply vector is currently undetectable, and the witness makes
it a measured refusal on qualified hardware.

**Attack 5 — "PDO names in the hashed body: machine-identifying bytes
in a plan artifact."** Refuted by company: SAFE-003's record already
carries the OS device instance identifier and connection path — the
epoch token is the same class, governed by the same SEC-006/SAFE-006
rules, and adds no category the record does not already contain.
